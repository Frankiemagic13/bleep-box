Bleep Box

Push a Stream Deck key. Your mic goes silent and a bleep plays on your stream. OBS gets it as its own mic source: Bleep Box Mic. You do not need a virtual audio cable.

OBS pluginAdds the Bleep Box Mic source to OBS.
Bleep Box appSettings, a large BLEEP button, and the hotkey Ctrl + Shift + B.
Stream Deck pluginThe Bleep key. Hold it to bleep.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • OBS Studio 30 or later
  • Stream Deck software 6.4 or later

1Install

  1. Close OBS.
  2. Copy this BleepBox folder to your computer (for example, to Downloads). If it is a zip file, right-click it and select Extract All.
  3. Double-click Install.bat.
  4. Windows asks: "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?" Click Yes.
    If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  5. The Stream Deck window opens. Click Install.
  6. In the black installer window, push Enter to close it.

2Set up OBS

  1. Open OBS.
  2. In the Sources box, click +. Select Bleep Box Mic. Click OK.
  3. Select your microphone. Set the input gain if necessary. Click OK.
  4. Find your old microphone in the Audio Mixer (for example "Mic/Aux"). Click its speaker icon to mute it, or remove it.
  5. To hear the bleep in your headphones: Edit → Advanced Audio Properties. On the Bleep Box Mic line, set Audio Monitoring to Monitor and Output.
Important: Do step 4. If the old mic source stays on, viewers hear your voice under the bleep.

3Set up Stream Deck

  1. Open the Stream Deck window.
  2. In the list on the right, find Bleep Box.
  3. Drag Bleep onto a key.
  4. Click the key to see its settings:
    • Mode: Hold to bleep (bleeps while you hold the key) or Press to start / stop.
    • Sound: Use app setting, or a sound for this key only. Mute only mutes the mic with no sound.
    • Volume: Use app volume, or a volume for this key only.

Tip: Make two keys, for example one with "Classic 1 kHz" and one with "Duck quack".

What the key shows

Ready.
Your mic is live.
Bleeping.
Your mic is muted.
Not connected.
Open OBS.

4Use the Bleep Box app

  • Open Bleep Box from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.
  • Hold the large BLEEP button, or hold Ctrl + Shift + B.
  • Select a bleep sound and set the bleep volume.
  • To use your own sound: click Choose .wav file….
  • You do not have to keep the app open. The Stream Deck key works when only OBS is open.

Problems

ProblemDo this
The app shows "OBS plugin not found".Open OBS. If it still shows the message, close OBS and do Install again.
The app shows "Add the source".Do step 2 (add Bleep Box Mic in OBS).
The app shows "Mic error".In OBS, double-click Bleep Box Mic and select a different microphone.
Viewers hear my voice during the bleep.Mute the old mic in OBS (step 2.4). In the app, turn on "Mute mic fully while bleeping".
The key shows APP OFF.Open OBS.
"Bleep Box Mic" is not in the OBS list.Close OBS. Do Install again. Then open OBS.
I hear my voice two times in my headphones.In Advanced Audio Properties, set the old mic to "Monitor Off".

Uninstall

  1. Close OBS.
  2. Double-click Uninstall.bat. Click Yes.
  3. In Stream Deck: Preferences → Plugins → Bleep Box → Uninstall.
